About Education Zone Kashmir: Authorized MBBS Consultants in Srinagar for Bangladesh
// About us

Admission consultants from Srinagar who work on one country, and know it well

Education Zone Kashmir is an authorized admission consultancy for MBBS in Bangladesh, based in Parray Pora, Srinagar. We do not sell ten countries. We work with 55 BM&DC-approved medical colleges in Bangladesh, know their fee structures, hostels and intake calendars, and place students from Jammu & Kashmir and the rest of India there every session.

// What we do

Everything between "should I?" and "I'm in class"

01Counselling and shortlist

A free session in Srinagar or online. We check NEET eligibility, understand the budget and the student, and narrow 55 colleges to three to five.

02Eligibility and equivalence

We file the DGHS equivalence application and handle the Bangladesh-side paperwork so the offer letter is not held up.

03Admission and offer letter

Application to the college, seat confirmation, and a written fee schedule before you pay the first installment — directly to the college account.

04Visa and travel

Student visa at the Bangladesh High Commission, tickets, and group travel for first-year students from Srinagar.

05Hostel and arrival

Airport pickup, hostel check-in, SIM card, bank account and registration with the Indian High Commission in Dhaka.

06Support through the course

A WhatsApp group per batch, a local coordinator who visits each semester, and FMGE orientation from the first year.

// How we work

Three rules we do not bend

One fee sheet

The figures on this website, in your counselling session and on the college's fee schedule are the same figures. If a college changes its fee, we update the sheet and tell you.

College fees go to the college

Tuition and hostel are paid to the college's own account against an official receipt. Our service charges are separate, explained in writing at the first meeting.

Honest no's

If a student is not eligible, or Bangladesh is not the right fit, we say so at the first meeting. A wrong admission costs a family five years.
