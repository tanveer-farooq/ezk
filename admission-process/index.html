MBBS in Bangladesh admission process, step by step

Six steps, roughly four months. For each one, here is what you do and what we do — so there are no surprises and no missed deadlines.

STEP 01 · WEEK 1Free counselling
You do

Bring (or send) your NEET scorecard, Class 10 and 12 marksheets and a rough budget. Come with the student — this is their decision.

We do

Check eligibility on the spot, explain the fee sheet line by line, and shortlist three to five colleges that fit budget, city preference and hostel needs.

STEP 02 · WEEKS 1–3Documents
You do

Collect the checklist below. Apply for a passport immediately if you do not have one — it is the single most common cause of delay.

We do

Verify every document, arrange attestation where needed and prepare the college application and DGHS equivalence file.

STEP 03 · WEEKS 3–6Application & equivalence
You do

Sign the application forms and the DGHS equivalence application. Nothing is paid to any college at this stage.

We do

File with the college and the Directorate General of Health Services in Dhaka, follow up, and share the equivalence certificate when it is issued.

STEP 04 · WEEKS 6–8Offer letter & first installment
You do

Review the offer letter and the written fee schedule. Pay the first installment to the college's bank account and keep the receipt.

We do

Confirm the seat with the college, obtain the admission letter and the visa recommendation letter, and explain the remaining installment dates.

STEP 05 · WEEKS 8–12Student visa
You do

Complete the medical tests, sign the visa application and attend the appointment at the Bangladesh High Commission or the nearest visa centre if required.

We do

Prepare the visa file, book the appointment, track the application and book travel once the visa is issued.

STEP 06 · WEEKS 12–16Travel, hostel & registration
You do

Fly with the group (Srinagar–Delhi–Dhaka or via Kolkata), settle into the hostel and start orientation.

We do

Airport pickup, hostel check-in, local SIM and bank account, registration with the college and the Indian High Commission, and your batch WhatsApp group.

// Documents required

The checklist

Originals plus two sets of self-attested copies. Bring them to counselling and we will tell you which need notarisation or apostille.

Academic
  • Class 10 marksheet and certificate
  • Class 12 marksheet and certificate (PCB + English)
  • NEET scorecard and admit card
  • School leaving / transfer certificate
  • Migration certificate, if issued
Identity & travel
  • Passport valid for at least 18 months
  • Aadhaar card
  • Birth certificate
  • 10 passport-size photographs, white background
  • Parents' ID proof
Financial & medical
  • Bank statement or sponsor letter for the first installment
  • Medical fitness certificate
  • HIV test report (for the student visa)
  • Police clearance certificate
  • Vaccination record
