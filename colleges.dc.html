How to read the fee figures

Total fee is the tuition for the full MBBS course, quoted by each college in US dollars and paid in installments over the course. First installment is what you pay at admission. Where hostel is "included", it is part of the total fee; "paid" means a monthly hostel charge; "not included" means you arrange accommodation, usually with our help. Use the sliders to set your budget, then compare up to three colleges side by side.

Which colleges are on this list

All 55 colleges are private medical colleges approved by the Bangladesh Medical & Dental Council and affiliated with a public university — University of Dhaka, Chittagong, Rajshahi, Sylhet Medical University, SHMU Khulna or the Bangladesh University of Professionals (Army Medical Colleges). Eight are women's colleges. We admit students to these colleges as authorized consultants; the list is updated each session.
