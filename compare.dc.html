Lowest total is not always cheapest

A college with hostel excluded can cost $4,000–6,000 more over five years than one that includes it. Compare the "Hostel" row with the total, and remember that a lower first installment helps cash flow but not the overall cost.

Distance changes daily life

Dhaka-city colleges mean easier flights home and more Indian students around; colleges 100–300 km out are usually cheaper and quieter, with larger campuses. Neither is better — it depends on the student.
