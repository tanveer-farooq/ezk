// The rules, in plain English

What Bangladesh and the NMC each require

NEET (NMC, India)

Mandatory for every Indian student who intends to practise in India after a foreign MBBS. You need to have qualified; the score itself is not used for admission in Bangladesh.

Marks (DGHS, Bangladesh)

Minimum GPA 3.5 in both Class 10 and Class 12 (7.0 combined) with GPA 3.5 in Biology — roughly 60% under the conversion table. Physics, Chemistry, Biology and English must be Class 12 subjects.

Age and gap

17 years by 31 December of the admission year. Class 12 should be passed within the last two years — for 2026–27 that means 2024 or later. Older results are considered case by case.

Rules summarised from the current DGHS/DGME notification for foreign students and the NMC's FMGL 2021 regulations. Both change from time to time; we re-check them at every counselling session. Read more in NEET eligibility for MBBS abroad.
