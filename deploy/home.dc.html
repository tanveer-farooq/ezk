// Common questions

Before you call

All 20 questions answered →
Is NEET compulsory for MBBS in Bangladesh?
Yes. The NMC requires every Indian student to have qualified NEET before joining an MBBS course abroad. You need to qualify, not top the exam — the result stays valid for three years.
What is the total cost including hostel?
Course fees run from $30,000 to $49,000 for the full MBBS, and most colleges include hostel. Budget an additional ₹1.5–2 lakh per year for food, books and travel.
Can I practise in India after MBBS from Bangladesh?
Yes, after clearing FMGE (being replaced by NExT) and completing the requirements under NMC's FMGL 2021 regulations. Bangladesh graduates are consistently among the strongest FMGE performers.
Do you charge a consultancy fee on top of college fees?
College fees are paid directly to the college. Our service charges are explained in writing at the first counselling session — nothing is added silently to the fee sheet.
When does the 2026–27 session start?
Classes typically begin between January and March; admission paperwork runs from September to December. Starting early gives you the widest choice of colleges.
