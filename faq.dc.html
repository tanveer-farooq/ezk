Eligibility

Is NEET compulsory for MBBS in Bangladesh?
Yes. The NMC requires every Indian student to have qualified NEET before joining MBBS abroad. You need to qualify, not top the exam; the result is valid for three years.
What marks do I need in Class 12?
At least 60% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry and Biology (GPA 3.5 on the Bangladesh scale), with 60% in Biology, and 60% in Class 10 for the combined GPA of 7.0. English must be a Class 12 subject. Use the eligibility checker.
Is there an age limit or gap-year rule?
You must be 17 by 31 December of the admission year. Bangladesh expects Class 12 passed within the last two years; older results are considered case by case.
Can students from a state board (JKBOSE) apply?
Yes. State board, CBSE and ICSE results are all accepted; the DGHS converts marks to its GPA scale during equivalence.

Fees & payments

What is the total fee for MBBS in Bangladesh?
For 2026–27 the total course fee ranges from $30,000 to $49,000 across the 55 colleges on our list, with a first installment between $10,500 and $26,000. See the college-wise fee sheet.
How are installments paid?
The first installment is paid at admission, directly to the college's bank account against a receipt. The balance is paid in yearly installments on a schedule set by the college and given to you in writing.
Is hostel included?
At most colleges, yes. Some charge hostel monthly ($75–160) and a few exclude it; the dashboard shows this per college. Food is usually separate.
Do you add a consultancy charge to the college fee?
No. College fees are what the college charges. Our service charge is separate and explained in writing at the first counselling session.
Are education loans available?
Several Indian banks offer loans for MBBS abroad against the college offer letter and fee schedule. We provide the documents banks ask for; loan approval is between you and the bank.

Recognition & FMGE

Is the degree recognised?
The colleges we work with are approved by the Bangladesh Medical & Dental Council, affiliated with public universities and listed in the World Directory of Medical Schools.
Can I practise in India afterwards?
Yes, after clearing FMGE (being replaced by NExT) and meeting the NMC's FMGL 2021 requirements — 54 months of study plus a 12-month internship, taught in English. Bangladesh graduates are consistently among the strongest FMGE performers.
Can I do postgraduation abroad?
A WDOMS-listed MBBS lets you sit USMLE, PLAB and similar exams, subject to each country's rules.

Life in Bangladesh

What is the medium of instruction?
English throughout. The syllabus follows the Indian pattern with the same standard textbooks.
Is it safe? What about food?
International students live in dedicated hostels with 24-hour security, separate blocks for girls, and the Indian High Commission registers every student. Food is rice-and-curry based and halal everywhere; Kashmiri students adapt within weeks.
How do students travel from Srinagar?
Srinagar–Delhi–Dhaka (about four hours in the air) or via Kolkata. We organise group travel for first-year students; most fly home twice a year.
