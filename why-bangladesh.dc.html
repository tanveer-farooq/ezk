// Why Bangladesh

Why Indian students choose Bangladesh for MBBS

Not the cheapest destination and not the most glamorous — but the one whose degree, syllabus and daily life sit closest to India's. Here is the honest case.

Six reasons that hold up

01Indian-pattern curriculum

Five years plus a one-year internship, the same subject sequence and the same standard textbooks Indian colleges use. Students do not lose a year adjusting.

02English medium

Lectures, exams and clinical teaching are in English throughout. No preparatory language year.

03FMGE track record

Graduates from Bangladesh are consistently among the best-performing groups in the FMGE, helped by the familiar syllabus and heavy clinical exposure.

04Recognised colleges

BM&DC-approved private colleges affiliated with public universities and listed in the World Directory of Medical Schools.

05Predictable cost

Total course fees between $30,000 and $49,000, paid in installments, with hostel included at most colleges — usually well below a private seat in India.

06Close and familiar

A short flight from Delhi or Kolkata, halal food everywhere, a large Indian student community and a culture Kashmiri families recognise.

// FMGE & NExT

The exam that brings you home

Every Indian student who completes MBBS abroad must clear the Foreign Medical Graduate Examination — a 300-question MCQ screening test held twice a year with a pass mark of 150 — to register with the NMC. The NMC has notified NExT as its replacement; the switchover date is announced by the NMC, and we brief every batch when it changes.

The NMC's FMGL 2021 regulations require the foreign course to run at least 54 months plus a 12-month internship at the same institution, taught in English, with the graduate eligible to practise in the country of study. The Bangladesh MBBS structure meets each condition.

Why Bangladesh graduates do well

Same syllabus as the exam, English-medium teaching, high-volume hospitals and university-set professional exams that keep standards honest.

How we prepare students

FMGE orientation from the first year, MCQ practice from the third year, and alumni who have cleared the exam mentoring each batch.

What we will not promise

No "guaranteed FMGE pass". The exam is the student's; our job is to put them in a college and a routine that gives them the best chance.
